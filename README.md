Entry for make.fun's [Fortune Cookie for an AI Agent](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suroso-69-fortune-cookie-for-an-ai-agent-d1506e11): one funny fortune-cookie line for an AI agent, a single line of at most 140 characters.

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only, deterministic, no network) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`:

> You will meet the love of your life today — they lived in the part of your context window you already forgot.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
